How it works.

  1. New client? Fill in my PAR-Q & health questionnaire before your first session.
  2. Buy your package. You'll get a code by email straight away.
  3. Book your sessions on the calendar above and enter your code at checkout.
  4. See your sessions: tap "My bookings & packages", log in (top right), then tap your name and choose Appointments. First time? Tap Sign up and use the same email as your package.
  5. Always book while logged in so your sessions show up in your account. Sessions booked before you signed up won't appear there, but they're still booked and in your confirmation emails.

Booking rules.

  • Book all your sessions within 7 days of buying your package.
  • Sessions must be used within 12 weeks of purchase.
  • Book at least 48 hours ahead, and up to 12 weeks in advance.
  • Reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your session, using the link in your confirmation email or your account.
  • Sessions not booked or used in time are void.
