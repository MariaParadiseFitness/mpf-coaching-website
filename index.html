What you'll get

Your online coaching, all in one place.

01

Clear goals + structured phases

We don't "wing it". Your journey is split into phases: fat loss, maintenance and growth. Each one has clear targets, a purpose and a direction, so you always know what you're working towards, and why.

02

Personalised training programme

  • Progression built in
  • Clear sets, reps, tempo, rest and RIR
  • Exercises that suit you, not a random plan
  • No more wandering the gym like a lost soul in leggings
03

Form guidance + video analysis

Send me your lifts any time for technique corrections, better execution and mind-muscle connection. So you train safely, ideally before your lower back files a formal complaint.

04

Full nutrition guidance + tracking lesson

This is where things really change. I don't just give you numbers, I teach you how to:

  • Track calories and macros properly
  • Build meals that support your goals
  • Eat out without feeling you've "failed"
  • Manage hunger and your mindset around food
05

Weekly check-ins

Every week we review your progress photos, weight trend, strength, steps, cardio, nutrition, sleep, stress and recovery. Then I adjust everything based on you. And yes, I will see the weekend in your check-in. I always see the weekend.

06

Online coaching hub

Your programme, check-ins, progress trackers, nutrition targets and resources, all organised in one Google Drive.

07

Training logbook system

You'll log your lifts every session, because real progress comes from getting stronger over time. "I think I lifted about that last time" isn't tracking, it's guessing with extra steps.

08

Ongoing support

Direct WhatsApp support, check-in feedback and an optional community group chat. You're not handed a plan and left to it. I reply faster than your ex ever did.

Your questions, answered

Before you ask…

I'm a beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Your programme spells out exactly what to do (sets, reps, tempo, rest), you can send me videos of your lifts for form feedback, and your nutrition lesson teaches you tracking from scratch. Nobody expects you to know it all on day one.

Do I need a gym?

Yes. Your programme is built around gym equipment so we can properly track your lifts and keep you progressing week after week.

How long do I need to commit?

Ideally a minimum of 3 months. Real, lasting change takes time, and that's long enough to build the habits and see results you can actually keep. The 6-day miracle plan is available elsewhere. It doesn't work there either.

What happens when I go on holiday?

Holidays are part of the coaching, not a break from it. We plan for them together based on your goals, so you can enjoy yourself without undoing your progress. No, you don't need to pack a food scale.

What if I have a bad week?

Tell me. That's what I'm here for. We look at what happened, adjust, and get you back on track. One bad week won't undo your progress. Giving up will.

What do I get when I start?

Your own online coaching hub, set up with your programme, trackers and targets, plus your full nutrition and tracking lesson, so you're ready to go from week one.

How much does it cost?

See the pricing options. Paying 3 or 6 months upfront skips the set-up fee and saves you the most. We can talk it all through on your free call.
