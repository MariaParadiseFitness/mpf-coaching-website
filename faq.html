Getting started

I'm a beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Your programme spells out exactly what to do (sets, reps, tempo, rest), you can send me videos of your lifts for form feedback, and your nutrition lesson teaches you tracking from scratch. Nobody expects you to know it all on day one.

Do you coach men and women?

Yes, both. I specialise in fat loss and body recomposition, whether you want to lose fat, build muscle or both.

I have an injury or a medical condition. Can I still train with you?

Yes, in most cases. Tell me about any injuries, medical conditions or medication on your sign-up form, and I'll build your training and nutrition around them. If you're under a doctor or physio, or you're unsure whether exercise is safe for you right now, check with them first and let me know what they say. I'm a coach, not a medic, so I'll always work alongside their advice.

What happens on the free call?

We chat about your goals, your history, your lifestyle and what's got in the way before. No pressure and no hard sell. If we're a good fit, I'll explain exactly how we'd work together. Book your free call.

Do I need a gym?

Yes. Your programme is built around gym equipment so we can properly track your lifts and keep you progressing week after week.

Online coaching

What do I get with online coaching?

Your own training programme, your calories and macros, a 1-hour nutrition lesson over the phone, weekly check-ins with detailed feedback from me, form checks on your lift videos, WhatsApp support and full access to the MPF app. See everything that's included.

How do check-ins work?

Once a week, on your check-in day, you upload your front, side and back photos and answer a few questions in the app. Your week's numbers (weight trend, steps, protein, training, sleep) are pulled in for you, and I send back detailed feedback that's saved so you can look back on it.

How much does it cost?

Monthly is £175 a month. 3 months is £495 (£165 a month) and 6 months is £900 (£150 a month), so paying ahead makes every month cheaper. There's a one-off £75 set-up fee. See the prices.

How long do I need to commit?

Ideally a minimum of 3 months. Real, lasting change takes time, and that's long enough to build the habits and see results you can actually keep. The 6-day miracle plan is available elsewhere. It doesn't work there either.

Can I message you between check-ins?

Yes. You can message me on WhatsApp, send me videos of your lifts for quick form feedback, and tell me how you're feeling so I can adjust things.

In-person PT

Where do you train clients?

At PureGym Charlton, south-east London.

How much is personal training?

Sessions come in packages, and the more sessions in your package, the less each one costs. See the PT prices.

How do I book, move or cancel a session?

Through the booking page. If you're a client, it's in your app under Training, then Book & manage sessions. To move or cancel, open the booking page and tap Log in at the top right with your email.

What if I need to cancel or move a session?

You can reschedule any time before your session, as long as the new time is still within your 2-month window. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, the session is taken from your package.

Do PT clients get the app too?

Yes, and it's free. In-person clients get the MPF app included, with your programme, logbook, daily targets and planner.

Food and nutrition

Will I have to give up foods I love?

No. I coach flexible dieting, so pizza nights, takeaways and eating out all fit. You learn how to make your food work for your goal instead of cutting things out.

I'm vegan or vegetarian. Can you still help?

Yes. Your targets and meal plans are built around how you eat, and the meal plan builder can make any meal vegan, vegetarian or pescatarian.

Do I have to track my food?

Tracking is how we make sure you're actually hitting your targets. In your nutrition lesson I teach you how to track properly using MyFitnessPal or your favourite calorie counter, so it becomes quick and easy.

Do you give meal plans?

Yes. You get a meal plan made for you, and inside the app you can generate new ones that hit your calories and macros, in 15 cuisines, with cooking instructions on every meal.

The MPF app

What is the MPF app?

Your coaching, all in one place on your phone: your programme, workout logbook, meal plans, daily targets, check-ins, progress photos and planner. Take the tour.

Do I download it from the App Store?

No. It runs from my website, so there's nothing to download. Save it to your home screen and it opens just like an app. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): tap the 3 dots, then Add to Home screen.

Can anyone use the app?

The MPF app is only for my coaching clients, online or in person. It unlocks when you become a member.

Are my progress photos private?

Yes. Only you and I can ever see them. They're stored privately with no public web address, they're never shared or posted without your permission, and you can ask me to delete them at any time.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Turn on calendar sync and your weekly plan shows in your own calendar, updating automatically.

Real life

What happens when I go on holiday?

Holidays are part of the coaching, not a break from it. We plan for them together based on your goals, so you can enjoy yourself without undoing your progress. No, you don't need to pack a food scale.

What if I have a bad week?

Tell me. That's what I'm here for. We look at what happened, adjust, and get you back on track. One bad week won't undo your progress.

I've tried everything before. Why would this be different?

Because it's built around your life, not a crash diet. We set realistic targets, adjust the plan instead of abandoning it, and you learn how to do this yourself, so you stop starting over every Monday.
