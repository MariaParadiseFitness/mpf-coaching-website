Chapter 1

Why we track

Tracking isn't about being perfect or never eating the foods you love. It's about knowing what you're actually eating, so we can make smart changes instead of guessing.

  • It shows us the truth. Most people underestimate what they eat by a lot. Tracking takes the guesswork out.
  • It lets me coach you properly. When your weight stalls, your log tells us why, so we fix the right thing.
  • It teaches you. After a few months you'll know roughly what's in most meals, even without the app.
The goal is consistent, honest and roughly right, not perfect. A slightly off day logged is far more useful than a "bad" day you didn't log.
Chapter 2

Set up the app

  1. Download MyFitnessPalFrom the App Store or Google Play. The free version is all you need. Premium is optional (see the questions at the end).
  2. Create your accountUse any email. When it asks for your goals, put in anything to get through. We'll change them in a minute.
  3. Set your unitsGo to More → Settings → Units and set weight to kilograms and energy to calories.
  4. Set your mealsIn Settings → Diary settings you can rename meals, for example Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks. Name them however you actually eat.
  5. Turn off exercise caloriesIn Settings → Diary settings, if you see an option about exercise calories, don't add them to your goal. My targets already allow for your training.

Chapter 4

How to log food

  1. Tap + then FoodPick the meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
  2. Search for the foodBe specific: "Tesco chicken breast raw" beats "chicken". Brand and supermarket names help.
  3. Pick a good entryChoose entries with a green tick where you can. Then check the numbers against the packet. If the calories or protein look wrong, pick another entry.
  4. Set the amount in gramsChange the serving size to 1 g and type in what your scales say, for example 150. This is the most accurate way.
  5. Tap the tick to add itThen carry on with the next food.
✅ Do: log as you go, or straight after each meal. It takes seconds when it's fresh.
❌ Don't: wait until bedtime and try to remember the whole day. That's where food goes missing.

Packaged food

On Premium you can scan the barcode. On the free version, search the product name and brand, then check the entry against the label. If you can't find it, use Create a food and copy the numbers off the packet per 100 g. You only have to do it once.

Home cooking

For meals you make often, use Recipes → Create a recipe. Add every ingredient (including the oil), say how many portions it makes, then log one portion. Next time it's one tap.

Chapter 5

Weighing your food

A cheap digital kitchen scale is the best £10 you'll spend on your progress. Eyeballing portions is where most tracking goes wrong.

  • Weigh in grams. Put the plate or bowl on, press zero (tare), add the food.
  • Raw or cooked: match the entry. Rice, pasta and meat change weight a lot when cooked. If you weigh it raw, pick a raw entry. If you weigh it cooked, pick a cooked entry. Raw is the most accurate.
  • Weigh the "little things". Oil, butter, peanut butter, sauces and dressings are the sneaky calories. A tablespoon of oil is around 120 kcal.
  • Spray oil counts too, but it's small. Log a few sprays.
  • Drinks count. Milk in coffee, juice, fizzy drinks that aren't zero, and alcohol all go in.
You don't have to weigh forever. Weigh carefully for the first few weeks so you learn what portions really look like.
Chapter 6

Save time every day

  • Copy meals. Eat the same breakfast most days? Tap the three dots next to the meal and copy it to today.
  • Save meals. Save your usual lunch or snack as a meal, so it's one tap.
  • Use Recent and Frequent. When you search, the tabs at the top show what you log most.
  • Plan ahead. Log tomorrow's food the night before, then just tweak it. It's the easiest way to hit your protein.
  • Quick add, only when you have to. Quick add lets you type in calories without a food. Use it for a meal out you can't find, not as your everyday method.
Chapter 7

Eating out, drinks and treats

  • Chains: most big chains (Nando's, Wagamama, Greggs, Pret and so on) are in the app. Search the restaurant name and the dish.
  • Independent restaurants: find the closest match and add a bit extra for the oil and butter restaurants use. Overestimating is better than underestimating.
  • Takeaways and buffets: log your best guess. A guess is still useful data.
  • Alcohol: search the drink and the size (for example "large glass red wine"). A big night can add 800 to 1,500 kcal.
  • Treats: they fit. Log them like everything else and plan the rest of the day around them.
Never skip logging because a day went "off plan". Those are the days I most need to see.
Chapter 8

Add your totals to the portal

This is the step that lets me coach you. Whether you train with me face to face or online, your daily totals go into your daily log in the portal.

  1. Find your totals in the appAt the bottom of your diary, tap Nutrition (or look at the totals row). You want calories, protein, carbs and fat for the day.
  2. Open your portalGo to Today → My daily log.
  3. Type in your totalsAdd your calories, protein, carbs and fat, plus your weight, steps, water and sleep.
  4. Tap saveDo this every day, ideally before bed. Missed a day? Tap the day in the row and fill it in.

Chapter 10

Questions

Do I need Premium?

No. Everything in this guide works on the free version. Premium adds the barcode scanner, macro goals in grams, different goals for different days and no adverts. It's nice to have, not essential.

Should I eat back my exercise calories?

No. Fitness trackers and the app usually overestimate them, and your targets from me already include your training. Ignore that number.

What if I go over my calories?

Log it honestly and carry on tomorrow. Don't try to "make up for it" by eating very little the next day. One day doesn't make or break your progress. Your weekly average is what counts.

I hit my calories but not my protein. Is that OK?

Protein is the most important number, especially when you're losing fat. Plan a protein source into every meal, and use easy options like Greek yoghurt, skyr, protein shakes, chicken, eggs or tofu.

Can I use a different app?

Yes. Any calorie tracking app is fine. The same rules apply, and you still add your totals to your portal.

I feel obsessive about tracking. What should I do?

Tell me. Tracking should help you, not stress you out. There are other ways I can coach you, and we'll find what works for you.

Stuck on anything?

Message me and I'll help you get it set up. That's what I'm here for.
