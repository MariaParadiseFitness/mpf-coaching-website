My aim for you

To make you fully independent.

I don't want you to need me forever. Everything I do is about teaching you, so you leave feeling confident in the gym and in control of your food: knowing how to train, how to progress, how to track and how to build meals that fit your life. Results you understand are results you keep.

That's why most of my clients start with more sessions per week, then gradually come down to one a week as they get more confident training on their own.

How it works, step by step

From your first chat to your first proper training session. I teach you the why first, so everything we do after makes sense.

1

Free consultation

In person at PureGym Charlton or over the phone. We talk about your goals, your history and whether we're the right fit. No pressure.

2

Pick your package and pay online

Choose the session block that suits you and pay securely online. Blocks are used within 12 weeks of purchase.

3

Make your client login + PAR-Q

Create your login in the Client Area and fill in your PAR-Q health questionnaire, so I know about any injuries or health needs before we start.

4

Book your sessions

Book, view and reschedule all your sessions yourself in the Client Area, any time.

5

Session 1: your nutrition lesson

How to set your calories and macros, track properly and diet flexibly, so it fits your life instead of taking it over.

6

Session 2: the science of building muscle

How hypertrophy training actually works: tension, effort, technique and progressive overload. You'll understand exactly why we train the way we do.

7

Then we train

Your programme is built around you and adapted to any injuries, illnesses, ability level or anything else you need. I write it in your training logbook and teach you how to progressively overload your weights.

📊

Plus: your progress tracker

Your own Google Sheets progress tracker for your weight, steps and progress, so we can both see exactly how far you've come.
