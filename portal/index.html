Save MPF to your phone 📲

It works just like an app. One tap from your home screen, no app store needed.

iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow, at the bottom)
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add. The MPF icon is now on your home screen
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap the 3 dots at the top right
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen
  4. Tap Install or Add. The MPF icon is now on your home screen

Can't see the options? You're probably inside Instagram or WhatsApp. Tap the 3 dots or the compass icon and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome first.

Always open MPF from the home screen icon. You'll stay logged in.
