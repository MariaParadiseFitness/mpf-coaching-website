Coaching terms and conditions

Last updated 21 November 2026

These terms apply to face to face personal training and online coaching with MPF Coaching (Maria Paradise, self-employed personal trainer). By booking, paying or using your client portal, you agree to them.

1. Your health

Before you start, you will complete a health questionnaire (PAR-Q). Please answer honestly and tell me straight away if anything changes, such as a new injury, illness, medication or pregnancy. If you have a medical condition or answer yes to any health question, I may ask you to get clearance from your GP before training.

Exercise and changes to your diet carry some risk. You take part at your own risk and agree to stop and tell me if you feel pain, dizziness or unwell. My coaching is not medical advice and does not replace advice from your doctor or another healthcare professional.

2. Online coaching: prices and payment

Every online coaching plan starts with a one-off £75 set-up fee. Then: monthly, £175 every month; 3 months, £495 every 3 months; or 6 months, £900 every 6 months. Payments are taken automatically by Stripe and each plan renews at the end of its period until you cancel.

A minimum of 3 months is recommended so you can see real results.

3. Online coaching: cancelling

You can cancel any plan by messaging me at least 7 days before your next payment date. Your coaching continues until the end of the period you have paid for, and no further payments are taken.

The set-up fee covers building your coaching plan and your nutrition lesson, and is non-refundable once work has started. Payments for a 3 or 6 month period are non-refundable once that period has started, except where the law says otherwise.

4. Face to face sessions and packages

Sessions are booked through Acuity Scheduling at PureGym Charlton. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to move or cancel a session. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, may be charged or taken from your package. Please arrive on time: late starts may not be extended.

Packages must be used within the time stated when you buy them. You need a valid gym membership to train with me in the gym.

5. What you can expect from me

A personalised training programme and nutrition targets, weekly check-ins (Sundays for face to face clients, your agreed day for online clients), feedback, and support by WhatsApp during reasonable working hours. Results depend on many things, including how consistently you follow the plan, so I cannot guarantee a specific result.

6. What I ask from you

Log your daily numbers in your portal, complete your weekly check-ins and photos on time, be honest with me, and let me know if you are struggling so we can adjust your plan.

7. Your client portal

Your portal is for your personal use. Please keep your login to yourself. Your training programme, meal plans and guides are for your own use and must not be shared or resold.

8. Your information and photos

How I use and protect your information is explained in my privacy policy. Your progress photos are private and are only ever shared on social media if you give permission in your portal, which you can change at any time.

9. Ending coaching

I may pause or end coaching if payments are missed, if I believe continuing would be unsafe for you, or if behaviour towards me is abusive. If I end coaching for a reason that is not your fault, I will refund any unused time you have paid for.

10. Changes and contact

I may update these terms from time to time, and I will tell current clients about any important changes. Questions? Email info@mpfcoaching.co.uk.

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. Nothing in them affects your legal rights as a consumer.
