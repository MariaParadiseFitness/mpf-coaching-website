Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains how MPF Coaching (Maria Paradise, self-employed personal trainer) collects, uses and protects your personal information, including the information you add to your client portal.

Who I am

MPF Coaching is run by Maria Paradise, who is the data controller for your information. For anything privacy-related, email info@mpfcoaching.co.uk.

What I collect and why

Free education sign-ups: your first name and email address, so I can give you my free guides, tools and recipes, your sign-up discount, and occasional tips and offers if you tick the box to agree. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Coaching applications, enquiries and bookings: what you enter into my forms, and the details you give when booking a call (Calendly) or a PT session (Acuity Scheduling), so I can arrange and run your coaching.

Your client portal: if you are a client, I keep the information you add to your portal so I can coach you. This includes your name, email, phone number, date of birth, job, goals, daily logs (such as weight, food totals, steps, sleep, training and how you feel), weekly check-ins, workout logs, your training programme and meal plan, your profile photo and your progress photos.

Health information

Some of what you share is health information, for example your health questionnaire (PAR-Q), medical conditions, medication, injuries, pregnancy, your menstrual cycle (if you choose to log it), any history of eating disorders, your measurements and your progress photos. UK data protection law treats this as special category data.

I only collect and use it with your explicit consent, which you give in your onboarding form or portal, and only so I can coach you safely and effectively. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing me. If you do, I will delete your health information, but I may then not be able to keep coaching you safely.

Your progress photos

Your photos are private. Only you and I can see them. They are kept in private, secure storage with no public web address, they cannot be found through search engines, and they are not saved on your device when you view them.

I will never post or share your photos without your consent. In your portal you choose whether I may share them on social media (for example @mpfcoachingresults). The answer is No unless you change it to Yes, and you can change it back at any time in My account. If you change it to No, I will not make any new posts, and I will remove posts I have made where I reasonably can.

How your information is kept safe

Your portal is protected by a login: you sign in with your email address and a one-time code. Each client can only see their own information. All data is sent over an encrypted connection.

Who else handles your data

I use trusted services to run my business. They process data on my behalf under their own privacy and security terms: Cloudflare (website hosting, secure login and storage of your portal data and photos), Google (email and forms), Calendly and Acuity Scheduling (bookings), Stripe (payments, if you pay online) and WhatsApp (if we message there). I never sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

Mailing-list details until you unsubscribe. Client information, including your portal data and photos, for as long as you are a client, and then for up to 2 years in case you return, unless you ask me to delete it sooner. Some records, such as payments, may need to be kept for longer for tax and insurance reasons.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, or stop me using it. You can also withdraw any consent you have given. Just email info@mpfcoaching.co.uk and I will respond within one month. Every marketing email also includes an unsubscribe link. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Calculators on this site

The calorie and macro calculator and other public tools run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to me or stored.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. When you log in to your portal, a secure login cookie keeps you signed in. It is needed for the portal to work and is not used for anything else.
