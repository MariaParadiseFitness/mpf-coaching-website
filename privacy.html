Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains how MPF Coaching (Maria Paradise, self-employed personal trainer) uses any personal information you share through this website.

Who I am

MPF Coaching is run by Maria Paradise. For anything privacy-related, email info@mpfcoaching.co.uk.

What I collect and why

Free guide sign-ups: your first name, email address and which guide you downloaded. I use these to send you the guide and occasional tips and offers, only if you tick the box to agree.

Coaching applications and enquiries: what you enter into my application or enquiry forms (Google Forms), so I can assess whether coaching is right for you and contact you about it.

Bookings: details you enter when booking a call (Calendly) or a PT session (Acuity Scheduling), so I can arrange and run the appointment.

Calculators on this site

The calorie and macro calculator and other tools run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to me or stored.

Who else handles your data

I use trusted services to run this site and my business: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google (forms and email), Calendly and Acuity Scheduling (bookings). They process data on my behalf under their own privacy terms. I never sell your information.

How long I keep it

Mailing-list details until you unsubscribe. Enquiry and client information for as long as I need it to provide coaching and meet legal and insurance requirements.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop receiving emails, at any time. Just email info@mpfcoaching.co.uk. Every marketing email will also include an unsubscribe link. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.
