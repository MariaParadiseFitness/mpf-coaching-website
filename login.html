How to log in to your portal
  1. Tap your portal above and type in your email (the one you gave Maria).
  2. You'll get an email from Cloudflare with a 6-digit code. Cloudflare is the secure login service we use, so this is from us.
  3. Type the code in and you're in. You stay logged in for a month.

🔒 Your information is private. Only you and Maria can see your portal, your logs and your progress photos. Photos are kept in private, secure storage with no public link, and are never shared or posted without your permission.

No email after a couple of minutes? Check your junk or spam folder, or message Maria.
